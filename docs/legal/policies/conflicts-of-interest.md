# Conflicts of interest

**Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** 1 January 2027

Uvalu publishes fair value estimates and model signals on listed shares. This page explains what interests we have that could influence those outputs, and how we manage them.

## 1. Who produces the signals

Signals and valuations are produced by **Sven Claes**, a sole trader (eenmanszaak) trading under the name Uvalu, Pol Heynsstraat 7, 2200 Herentals, Belgium, enterprise number [TBD: ENTERPRISE NUMBER — not yet registered].

Person involved in designing the models: Sven Claes, founder and developer.

Uvalu is not an investment firm and is not authorised or supervised by the FSMA to provide investment services.

## 2. How signals are made

Signals are calculated automatically, with the same formulas and thresholds for every stock we cover. Nobody at Uvalu sets or overrides the signal of an individual stock. The rules are published on the Methodology page.

## 3. Our own investments

Uvalu is run by its founder as a sole trader, so the founder's own investments are the only relevant positions. The founder invests privately in listed shares, which may include shares that Uvalu covers.

We disclose on the stock page of the instrument concerned when the founder holds a net long or short position above 0.5% of the issuer's share capital. Below that threshold, positions may exist without a notice on the stock page.

## 4. Rules we apply to our own trading

- **No trading ahead of model changes.** When a change to models or thresholds is planned that would alter signals, the founder does not trade the affected shares from the moment the change is decided until 2 trading days after it is live.
- **No override.** Signals of shares held by the founder are calculated exactly like all others.
- **No promotion of own positions without disclosure.** When we mention an individual share in marketing, newsletters or social media, we always state whether the founder holds it.
- **Trade register.** The founder keeps a dated record of personal transactions in covered shares, which can be given to the regulator on request.

## 5. Payments and relationships

- **Issuers.** We receive no payment or other benefit from companies whose shares we cover, and they have no say in our outputs. They do not see a signal before it is published.
- **Brokers and platforms.** We receive no commission, referral or affiliate fee from brokers, banks or trading platforms. Uvalu contains no order buttons or broker links.
- **Data providers.** We pay our data providers for data. They do not influence our models or signals.
- **Advertising.** Uvalu shows no third-party advertising.
- **Our only income** is subscription fees paid by users.

If any of the above changes, we update this page before the change takes effect.

## 6. Specific disclosures

Where a specific conflict exists for a share, it is shown on that share's page. At the date of this version: none.
