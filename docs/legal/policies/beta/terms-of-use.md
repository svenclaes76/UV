## 3. What Uvalu is

Uvalu is an online analysis tool for private investors. It lets you:

- calculate fair value estimates for listed shares using several valuation models;
- see scores and model signals based on those estimates;
- record your own portfolio and view risk analytics for it.

## 4. What Uvalu is not

This section is important. Please read it carefully.

- **No investment advice.** Uvalu does not give personal investment advice. Scores, fair values and signals are generated automatically from public data and model settings. They are the same for every user and do not take your financial situation, goals, knowledge or risk tolerance into account.
- **No regulated investment service.** Uvalu is not an investment firm, bank or broker and is not authorised or supervised by the Belgian Financial Services and Markets Authority (FSMA) to provide investment services.
- **No execution or custody.** Uvalu does not place orders, hold money or hold securities for you. Positions and trades you enter are your own records.
- **No tax advice.** Any tax figures are estimates based on the rules described in the app. You remain responsible for your own tax return.
- **No guarantee of results.** Model outputs can be wrong. Past performance and historical simulations do not predict future returns. Investing involves risk, including loss of capital.

You make your own investment decisions and you are responsible for them. Where appropriate, consult an authorised adviser.

- Risk figures such as VaR, CVaR and Monte Carlo results are statistical estimates based on historical data and assumptions. Real losses can be larger.
