# Complaints and Consumer Mediation Service

**Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** 1 January 2027

If something went wrong, tell us. Most problems can be solved quickly and directly.

## 1. Contact us first

- Email: complaints@uvalu.app
- Post: Sven Claes (Uvalu), Pol Heynsstraat 7, 2200 Herentals, Belgium

Please include:

- the email address of your account;
- what happened and when;
- what you would like us to do;
- screenshots or invoice numbers, if relevant.

You can write in Dutch, English, French, German, Italian or Spanish.

## 2. What you can expect

| Step | When |
| --- | --- |
| We confirm we received your complaint | Within 2 working days |
| We send you our answer, with reasons | Within 14 calendar days |
| If we need more time, we tell you why and when you will hear from us | Before the 14 days end; final answer within 30 days at most |

Making a complaint is free and does not affect your account or your legal rights.

## 3. What we cannot help with

Uvalu does not give personal investment advice. We can look into errors in data, calculations, billing or the working of the service. We cannot tell you whether to buy, hold or sell an investment, and we do not compensate investment results. See the Terms of use, sections 3 and 12.

## 4. If you are not satisfied: Consumer Mediation Service

If you are a consumer and we could not solve your complaint, you can ask the Belgian Consumer Mediation Service for an out-of-court solution. It is independent and free for consumers.

**Consumer Mediation Service** (Consumentenombudsdienst / Service de Médiation pour le Consommateur)
North Gate II, Koning Albert II-laan 8, 1000 Brussels, Belgium
Phone: +32 2 702 52 00
Email: contact@consumentenombudsdienst.be
Website: [www.consumentenombudsdienst.be](https://www.consumentenombudsdienst.be)

The service normally asks that you first tried to solve the matter with us.

If you live in another EU country, the European Consumer Centre in your country can also help you with a cross-border complaint: [www.eccnet.eu](https://www.eccnet.eu).

## 5. Other bodies

- **Privacy.** Complaints about how we handle personal data can go to the Belgian Data Protection Authority. See the Privacy notice, section 9.
- **Unfair commercial practices.** You can report these to the Belgian Economic Inspection through [meldpunt.belgie.be](https://meldpunt.belgie.be).
- **Financial ombudsman.** Uvalu is not a bank, broker or investment firm, so Ombudsfin does not handle complaints about Uvalu.

## 6. Going to court

You always keep the right to go to court. See the Terms of use, section 16, for the applicable law and the competent courts.
