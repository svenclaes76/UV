# Expert Profile: Accountant / Tax Advisor

**Project:** Uvalu — stock screening and portfolio tool for solo retail investors
**Priority:** High — needed before the first paid subscription

---

## Why Uvalu needs this expert

Uvalu will sell digital subscriptions to consumers across the EU. That triggers VAT at the customer's location (via the One-Stop-Shop), requires a choice of legal form in Belgium, and determines how subscription income — including trial conversions and refunds — is booked and taxed.

## Required expertise

- Belgian certified accountant and/or tax advisor (member of the **ITAA**, Institute for Tax Advisors and Accountants)
- **EU VAT on electronically supplied services**: place-of-supply rules, the EU-wide €10,000 threshold, **OSS registration and quarterly returns**, evidence of customer location, VAT on B2B vs. B2C sales
- Belgian VAT specifics, including the small-business exemption scheme and how it interacts with OSS
- Belgian **legal forms**: sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), BV/SRL, management company structures; social-security status of a self-employed founder (including as secondary activity alongside a job)
- Revenue recognition for subscriptions (monthly billing, free trials), payment-provider fees, refunds and chargebacks
- Familiarity with payment platforms acting as merchant of record (e.g. Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) vs. direct billing (e.g. Stripe, Mollie)

## Scope of review

1. **Legal form:** sole proprietorship vs. BV — liability (important given financial-advice risk), tax rate, costs, administration, timing of incorporation
2. **Main vs. secondary activity:** social-contribution implications if Uvalu starts alongside other income
3. **VAT set-up:** VAT number, small-business scheme eligibility, OSS registration, invoicing rules, VAT-inclusive pricing per country
4. **Merchant of record:** whether to keep Mollie or switch to a reseller platform that handles EU VAT, and the trade-off in fees vs. admin
5. **Subscription income:** recognition of monthly plans, the 30-day free trial, refunds after withdrawal
6. **Deductible costs:** data licences, hosting, software, home-office, equipment, professional fees
7. **Intellectual property:** whether IP should sit in the company; Belgian innovation income deduction relevance for software
8. **Bookkeeping workflow:** tools, invoicing integration, reporting calendar

## Expected deliverables

- Recommendation on legal form and timing, with a cost comparison
- VAT and OSS set-up checklist and filing calendar
- Recommendation on billing platform (merchant of record vs. own billing)
- Bookkeeping workflow and chart of accounts for subscription revenue
- Annual cost estimate for accounting services

## Material to prepare

- Expected pricing, plans and number of subscribers in years 1–3
- Target countries (EU and non-EU)
- Current employment / income status
- Expected costs (EODHD, hosting, legal, design)
- Payment provider: Mollie (chosen), monthly billing only

## Questions to ask when selecting

- How many SaaS or digital-product clients selling EU-wide do you handle?
- Have you set up OSS for clients and filed OSS returns?
- Can you advise on legal form, social status and VAT in one engagement?

## Red flags

- Unfamiliar with OSS or with place-of-supply rules for digital services
- No digital bookkeeping tools or integrations
- Advises on legal form without looking at the liability risk of the product

## Where to find

- ITAA member directory
- Start-up accounting offices in Flanders specialising in digital businesses
- Referrals from other Belgian SaaS founders
