# Expert Profile: UX Researcher (with Target-User Recruitment)

**Project:** Uvalu — stock screening and portfolio tool for solo retail investors
**Priority:** High — after the redesign's first screens are usable, before launch

---

## Why Uvalu needs this expert

Uvalu has been redesigned across its main screens (Dashboard, Screener, Watchlist, Portfolio, Cash, Risk, Analysis). The designer and developer know the product too well to judge it objectively. Real retail value investors need to try it, so it is clear whether they understand fair value, margin of safety, model signals, the quality screen and risk bands — and, importantly, whether they misread signals as instructions to buy.

## Required expertise

- 5+ years of UX research, with **moderated usability testing**, contextual interviews and task-based evaluation
- Experience researching **fintech or investing products** and financially literate audiences
- Ability to **recruit target users**: self-directed retail investors in Belgium / the Netherlands / the EU, especially value-oriented investors
- Comfortable running sessions in **Dutch and English** (French a plus)
- Knowledge of comprehension testing for numbers and risk (e.g. do users understand VaR, ranges, uncertainty)
- GDPR-compliant handling of research recordings and consent

## Scope of research

1. **Participant profile:** 6–10 retail investors per round, mix of experience levels and portfolio sizes, all managing their own investments
2. **Core tasks:** find undervalued stocks in the Screener, interpret the Analysis (stock detail) page, add holdings, read the Risk screen
3. **Comprehension:** what users think fair value, margin of safety, Undervalued / Mid score / Low score / Screened out and the risk bands mean; how much they trust the numbers
4. **Risk of misinterpretation:** whether signals are read as personal advice (input for the financial-regulation lawyer)
5. **Methodology page:** do users find and understand it?
6. **Onboarding and pricing:** willingness to pay, which features justify a subscription
7. **Navigation and terminology** across the redesigned shell

## Expected deliverables

- Research plan and screener questionnaire for recruitment
- Session guide with tasks
- Recordings and notes (with consent)
- Findings report with severity-rated issues and recommendations
- Short video highlight reel of key moments
- Recommendations on terminology and labels

## Material to prepare

- Working prototype or staging environment with realistic data
- Redesign screens and implementation plan
- Research questions and hypotheses Sven wants answered
- Budget for participant incentives

## Questions to ask when selecting

- Have you tested investing or banking products before?
- How will you recruit retail investors in Belgium specifically?
- How do you test comprehension of numerical and risk concepts?

## Red flags

- Proposes only a survey or unmoderated click tests
- Uses colleagues or friends as participants instead of real target users
- Leads participants or explains the interface during tasks

## Where to find

- Freelance UX researchers in Belgium / the Netherlands with fintech portfolios
- Recruitment through investor communities (e.g. Belgian investor associations, value-investing forums and groups) with consent
- UX research agencies offering recruitment plus moderation
