# Uvalu.app Roadmap: Q4 2026 Objectives & 2027 Plan
*Operationalizing [v2](strategic-business-plan-v2.md) (current mode) toward [v3](strategic-business-plan-v3.md) (the goal).*

---

## How This Fits

This isn't a new strategy — it's the concrete, dated execution plan for the strategy already decided:
- **[v2](strategic-business-plan-v2.md)** is still how you're operating: free time, personal use first, zero
  external spend, no pressure.
- **[v3](strategic-business-plan-v3.md)** is the destination you want to move toward: full-time,
  ~€9,000/month MRR, ~600 paying users.
- **This document** answers "so what do I actually do, and by when, to close that gap" — starting with
  **Phase 0** of v3 (prove real demand exists) while staying inside v2's constraints.

Nothing here changes v2's time budget (5-10 hrs/week, adjust as needed) or its bootstrapped/no-pressure
posture. It just gives that time direction.

## 1. Q4 2026 Objectives (Now → Dec 31, 2026)

~15 weeks left in 2026. At v2's 5-10 hrs/week, that's roughly 75-150 hours total — a real but modest budget,
comparable in total effort to the 40-day sprint that shipped v1.1.0-v1.9.0, just spread out instead of
concentrated. Five objectives, in priority order:

### Objective 1 — Ship a minimal paid tier (the biggest lift, and the actual prerequisite for everything else)
**Reality check first**: there is currently **no payment or subscription infrastructure in the codebase at
all** (confirmed — `admin.py` explicitly notes "this app has no subscription/plan concept"). v3's Phase 0
("turn on monetization for real") can't start without building this. Scope it minimally:
- Gate 1-2 already-built features behind a "Pro" flag (e.g. unlimited saved screens, multiple portfolios,
  full risk detail) — reuse existing role/auth infrastructure from the v1.8.0 auth overhaul rather than
  building new access control from scratch.
- Stripe Checkout (hosted page, not a custom payment form) for the simplest possible integration — a few
  hours of work, not a project.
- Basic receipt/invoice via Stripe's own emails — no custom billing UI needed at this scale.
- **Done when**: you could plausibly ask a real person to pay and it would actually work end to end.

### Objective 2 — A minimal disclaimer + ToS update before inviting anyone who might pay
Not the full legal review v3 budgets for a public launch — just a clear, honest "informational tool, not
investment advice" statement and a lightweight terms-of-service update, appropriate for a small invited
circle. Revisit properly (real legal review) only if/when Phase 1's public launch becomes real.
- **Done when**: anyone you invite sees this before they can pay.

### Objective 3 — Keep using it for real investing decisions
The existing v2 milestone, made concrete: by Dec 31, 2026, can you point to at least one real investing
decision (a buy, a sell, a pass) that Uvalu actually informed? If not, that's more important signal than
anything else on this list — fix that before spending more time on GTM-adjacent work.

### Objective 4 — Grow the invited circle to 5-15 people, and actually ask
Invite trusted people (friends, forum contacts) per v2 Section 5. The new part: once Objective 1 ships,
explicitly ask a few of them "would you pay for this" — not a survey, just honest conversations. This is
where v3's Phase 0 gate ("a few dozen real paying customers acquired with minimal marketing effort") starts
to get real evidence, even if you land at a handful rather than dozens by year-end. Reaching the full v3
gate is a 2027 goal, not a Q4 2026 one — see [Section 3](#3-2027-high-level-plan).

### Objective 5 — Start a build-in-public habit (near-zero cost, compounds into 2027)
Per v3 Section 5's GTM idea: a short post whenever something ships (a changelog entry turned into a public
post — X/LinkedIn/personal blog, whichever is genuinely low-effort for you). This costs almost no extra time
on top of the release notes you already write, and it's the cheapest possible seed for 2027's content/SEO
work.

### Explicitly not a Q4 2026 objective
- Incorporation, hiring, paid ads, mobile app, public API, migrating off yfinance — all correctly gated to
  later per v2/v3, no reason to pull any of them forward.

## 2. Roadmap Clarification — What's Near-Term vs. Gated

v2 and v3 each have their own "what's in scope" list; here's the merged, timed view:

| Item | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Paywall + Stripe (Objective 1) | **Q4 2026** | Literally can't test Phase 0 without it |
| Disclaimer/ToS update (Objective 2) | **Q4 2026** | Cheap, honest, appropriately scaled to invite-only |
| Build-in-public habit (Objective 5) | **Q4 2026, ongoing into 2027** | Near-zero cost, compounds |
| Real content/SEO cadence, public launch post | **2027 (Phase 1)** | Needs Phase 0 evidence first — no point driving traffic at an unproven funnel |
| Capped, self-funded paid ads | **2027 (Phase 1), only if organic funnel already converts** | v3's own rule: never fund ads from savings, only from existing MRR |
| Paid market-data provider (off yfinance) | **Only once revenue justifies it** — plausibly late 2027 at the earliest | Still a hypothetical cost until there's real paying-customer volume depending on uptime |
| Full legal review of MiFID II posture | **Gated to the actual public launch**, not before | Right-sized: a Q4 2026 disclaimer is enough for an invited circle; real legal spend waits until real public exposure |
| Belgian BV incorporation | **Gated to the Phase 1→2 transition** (v3 Section 6) | Premature before there's recurring revenue to protect |
| Mobile app, public API | **Evidence-gated, not scheduled** | Only revisit if paying users specifically ask and retention data supports it |
| First hire | **Phase 3 only, if ever** | Per v3 — not a 2026/2027 question at all |

The pattern: **2026 is entirely about building the one missing piece (payment) and starting to collect real
evidence.** 2027 is about acting on that evidence at a measured pace — nothing here compresses v3's own gates.

## 3. 2027 High-Level Plan

This is deliberately high-level — v3's own Decision Gates ([Section 9](strategic-business-plan-v3.md#9-decision-gates-not-a-fixed-roadmap))
already define the real triggers; 2027 is when most of Phase 0 and Phase 1 actually happen.

### Early 2027 (Q1) — Close out Phase 0
- Evaluate against v3's Phase 0 gate: real paying customers acquired with minimal marketing. If Q4 2026's
  handful of invited conversations produced a few paying users, this phase may already be close to done;
  if not, keep iterating on positioning/pricing with the invited circle before scaling anything up.
- **Do not rush this by adding marketing spend or effort** — the gate is about validating the offer works
  at all, not about volume yet.

### Mid 2027 (Q2-Q3) — Phase 1 begins: build the acquisition engine
- Sustained content/SEO cadence (biweekly-ish, whatever's realistic alongside a day job).
- One well-prepared public launch post (Show HN / relevant investing community) — a single event, not an
  ongoing campaign.
- Start tracking the real funnel numbers against v3 Section 1's planning assumptions (€15 ARPU, ~3%
  conversion) — this is the first real data point on whether those assumptions are even close, and they'll
  very likely need adjusting once real numbers come in.
- Small, capped paid-ad experiments **only if** organic shows signs of converting, funded strictly from
  whatever MRR already exists.

### Late 2027 (Q4) — First honest read on trajectory
- v3's Phase 1 gate is a 3+ consecutive month MRR trend, not a single-year deadline — so **don't expect to
  hit the €6,500-7,000/month transition threshold by end of 2027**; that's an unrealistic bar for the first
  full year of real GTM effort on a side-project schedule, and treating it as one risks a false sense of
  failure.
- What end of 2027 *should* tell you: is MRR growing month over month at all, is the funnel converting
  anywhere near the planning assumptions, and does the whole thing still feel worth continuing. That's
  enough to decide whether 2028 is "keep pushing toward the gate" or "recalibrate/extend" per v3's own
  no-go rule (which allows 18-24 months from Phase 1's start — i.e., into 2028-2029, not a 2027 cliff).

### What doesn't happen in 2027, on current evidence
Incorporation, hiring, a data-provider migration, mobile/API work — all still gated per
[Section 2](#2-roadmap-clarification--whats-near-term-vs-gated) to signals that, realistically, a single year
of part-time GTM work is unlikely to produce. If evidence comes in faster than expected, great — revisit
gates as they're actually met, not on a calendar.

## 4. Check-in Cadence

Reuses v2/v3's own rhythm rather than inventing a new one:
- **End of Q4 2026** (Dec 2026): review the five objectives in [Section 1](#1-q4-2026-objectives-now--dec-31-2026) —
  which shipped, which didn't, and why. Adjust Q1 2027 plans honestly based on what actually happened, not
  what was hoped.
- **Quarterly through 2027** (Mar / Jun / Sep / Dec): track MRR, registered users, and conversion against
  v3 Section 1's planning numbers; update this document's assumptions as real data replaces guesses.
- **Any time**: if it stops being fun, or personal use (Objective 3) stops being true, that's a legitimate
  reason to slow down or fall back toward pure v2 mode — carried forward from both v2 and v3's own stated
  philosophy.
